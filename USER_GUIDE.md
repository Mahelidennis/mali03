## 4. SMS Integration

### 4.1 What is SMS Integration?
Mali can automatically track your M-PESA transactions by reading SMS messages from your phone. This eliminates manual data entry and gives you a complete picture of your finances.

### 4.2 How It Works
1. **Permission Request**: Mali asks for SMS reading permission
2. **Message Processing**: Only M-PESA messages are processed
3. **Data Extraction**: Transaction details are extracted automatically
4. **Categorization**: Expenses are automatically categorized
5. **Insights**: Mali provides insights based on your spending

### 4.3 Setting Up SMS Tracking

#### Step 1: Grant Permission
1. When prompted, tap "Enable Automatic Tracking"
2. Read the explanation of how SMS data is used
3. Tap "Allow" when your phone asks for SMS permission
4. Mali will start scanning for M-PESA transactions

#### Step 2: Review Settings
1. Go to Settings > SMS Integration
2. Choose which accounts to track
3. Set data retention preferences
4. Configure notification settings

### 4.4 What Data is Collected
- **Transaction Amount**: How much money was involved
- **Recipient**: Who you sent money to or received from
- **Date and Time**: When the transaction occurred
- **Transaction Type**: Send, receive, buy goods, pay bills, etc.

### 4.5 Privacy and Security
- **Local Processing**: SMS messages are processed on your device
- **No Raw Storage**: Raw SMS content is never stored
- **Encrypted Storage**: Only processed data is stored securely
- **Your Control**: You can disable SMS tracking anytime

### 4.6 Managing SMS Data
- **View Transactions**: See all detected M-PESA transactions
- **Edit Categories**: Correct automatic categorizations
- **Delete Data**: Remove specific transactions or all SMS data
- **Export Data**: Download your transaction history

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### 14.1 General Questions

**Q: Is Mali free to use?**
A: Yes, Mali offers a free tier with core features. Premium features are available with a subscription.

**Q: Is my financial data safe?**
A: Yes, we use bank-level encryption and security measures to protect your data.

**Q: Can I use Mali without SMS integration?**
A: Yes, you can use Mali with manual data entry, though SMS integration makes it much easier.

**Q: Does Mali work offline?**
A: Some features work offline, but you need internet for AI chat and data sync.

### 14.2 SMS Integration Questions

**Q: What SMS messages does Mali read?**
A: Only M-PESA and mobile money transaction messages. We don't read personal messages.

**Q: Can I disable SMS tracking?**
A: Yes, you can disable SMS tracking anytime in settings.

**Q: Is my SMS data stored in the cloud?**
A: No, SMS messages are processed locally and only transaction data is stored.

**Q: Can I delete my SMS data?**
A: Yes, you can delete all SMS-related data anytime.

### 14.3 Technical Questions

**Q: Why isn't Mali responding to my messages?**
A: Check your internet connection and try restarting the app.

**Q: How do I backup my data?**
A: Use the export feature in settings to download your data.

**Q: Can I use Mali on multiple devices?**
A: Yes, your data syncs across all your devices when signed in.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" feature on the login screen.
