# Mali Financial Assistant - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 2024**

## 1. Introduction

Mali Financial Assistant ("Mali", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, profile details
- **Financial Data**: Income, expenses, budgets, financial goals
- **Transaction Data**: M-PESA and mobile money transactions (with your explicit consent)

### 2.2 SMS Data (M-PESA Integration)
- **Transaction Messages**: M-PESA confirmation messages
- **Amounts**: Transaction amounts and dates
- **Recipients**: Merchant names and transaction descriptions
- **Transaction Types**: Send, receive, buy goods, pay bills, withdrawals

### 2.3 Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, feature usage, session duration
- **Log Data**: Error logs, performance metrics, crash reports

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Financial Services
- **Budget Tracking**: Monitor your spending against budgets
- **Goal Management**: Track progress toward financial goals
- **Insights Generation**: Provide personalized financial recommendations
- **Transaction Categorization**: Automatically categorize your expenses

### 3.2 SMS Data Processing
- **Transaction Parsing**: Extract financial data from M-PESA messages
- **Spending Analysis**: Analyze your spending patterns
- **Automated Categorization**: Categorize transactions automatically
- **Financial Insights**: Generate personalized financial advice

### 3.3 Service Improvement
- **App Enhancement**: Improve app functionality and user experience
- **Feature Development**: Develop new features based on usage patterns
- **Bug Fixes**: Identify and resolve technical issues

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage
- **Device Storage**: Financial data stored locally on your device
- **Encryption**: Sensitive data encrypted using industry-standard methods
- **Access Control**: Data protected by device security features

### 4.2 Cloud Storage
- **Firebase**: Account information and preferences stored securely
- **Encryption**: All cloud data encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls**: Strict access controls and authentication

### 4.3 SMS Data Handling
- **Local Processing**: SMS messages processed locally on your device
- **No Raw Storage**: Raw SMS content never stored in cloud
- **Processed Data Only**: Only extracted transaction data stored
- **User Control**: You can delete SMS data at any time

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We Do NOT Share
- **Personal Financial Data**: Never shared with third parties
- **SMS Content**: Raw SMS messages never shared
- **Transaction Details**: Individual transaction data not shared
- **User Behavior**: Personal usage patterns not shared

### 5.2 Limited Sharing
- **Service Providers**: Trusted partners for app functionality only
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or legal process
- **User Consent**: Only with your explicit written consent

### 5.3 Aggregated Data
- **Anonymous Analytics**: General usage statistics (no personal data)
- **App Improvement**: Technical metrics for service enhancement
- **Research**: Anonymized data for financial research (with consent)

## 6. Your Rights and Controls

### 6.1 Data Access
- **View Your Data**: Access all your stored information
- **Export Data**: Download your financial data
- **Data Portability**: Transfer data to other services

### 6.2 Data Control
- **Delete Data**: Remove your data at any time
- **Update Information**: Correct or update your information

### 6.3 SMS Permissions
- **Grant/Revoke**: Control SMS access permissions

## 8. Data Retention

### 8.2 SMS Data

### 8.3 Technical Data

- **Crash Reports**: Retained for 1 year for app improvement
